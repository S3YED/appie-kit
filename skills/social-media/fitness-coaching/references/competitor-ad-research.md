## Funnel Patterns Observed
1. **Free call** (most common) — Lowest barrier, highest coach time cost
2. **Quiz funnel** (JM Transformations) — Pre-qualifies leads, collects data
3. **$1 trial** (Over 40 Alpha) — High-pressure, immediate commitment
4. **Lead magnet → DM** (Curtis High) — Low-friction, scalable
5. **Challenge upsell** — Low-cost challenge → full coaching (MAD Coaching)

## Winning Angles
- Mindset + body combined (mental resilience alongside training)
- "Serious inquiries only" / scarcity framing
- Identity shift ("become the man who...")
- Specific niche targeting (dads, over 40, busy professionals)
