# Lead Profile Notes — Call Triage Record

After each triage call, Ibrahim provides structured notes. This template captures Ibrahim's exact format.

## Template

```
[LEAD NAME]

[Demographics] — age, location, occupation, family situation.

[Current state] — weight/physique/health stats, training history, current routine.

[Health context] — medical issues, injuries, conditions, medications, age-related concerns.

[Why now] — specific trigger event that made them act. Not vague. Date or moment.

[What they've tried before] — previous coaches, programs, what worked/didn't, why they stopped.

[Blockers] — what actually stops them (not what they think stops them):
- Schedule reality (travel, work, family)
- Financial situation
- Past trauma with coaching
- Knowledge gaps
- External factors (culture, environment, family pressure)

[What they need from you] — what specifically they're looking for in a coach. Not general "accountability" — specific.

[Commitment level] — cold / warm / hot / very hot. Evidence:
- Have they followed through before?
- Are they making excuses or taking responsibility?
- Can they pay?

[Avoid self-sabotage triggers] — what would make them regress (travel, stress, routine disruption).

[Closing angle] — the one thing to hit on the second call to close them.

[Qualified?] — Yes / Maybe / No — and why.
```

## Pattern Summary

Ibrahim's note style is:
- **Short staccato sentences.** Not full prose. Fragments are fine.
- **Bullet points of insight**, not paragraphs of narrative.
- **Outcome first** — start with who they are and what they want.
- **Blockers second** — what's in their way (internal AND external).
- **Why you specifically** — why Ibrahim/the coaching is the answer for them.
- **Gut feel last** — one-line verdict with emoji tag.
