# Giveaway Lead Management Process

Template for VA/assistant handoff. Tiers leads by commitment score and manages WhatsApp outreach on a per-day schedule.

## Strategy: Two-Call Funnel

Ibrahim runs a **two-call sequence** for all Tier 1-2 leads:

1. **Triage Call (during giveaway, ~15 min):** Learn about the person. Understand their situation, why now, what's at stake. No pitch, no close. Does NOT call it a "qualification call" — frames it as "shortlisting" for the winner selection. Builds know/like/trust so the second call is easier.

2. **Sales Call (after giveaway ends):** The winner is announced. Everyone else gets called back with: "You didn't win, but here's the offer." Ibrahim pivots based on what he learned in the triage call — low-ticket ($99/mo HIM) or high-ticket (1:1 coaching at $500 off giveaway discount).

**VA role stops at booking the triage call.** Ibrahim handles both calls personally. VA never discusses pricing, never pitches, never promises anything.

## The Three Tiers

| Tier | Score | Action | Speed |
|------|-------|--------|-------|
| Tier 1 | 10/10 | Call invite Day 3-4, value drops, final check Day 12-13 | Fast |
| Tier 2 | 8-9/10 | Check-in Day 3-4, call invite Day 5-6 | Medium |
| Tier 3 | 7 and below | Value only, pivot to low-ticket after giveaway ends | Nurture |

## Where Leads Come From

- Daily Telegram summary at 9am Dubai time from the automated system
- Ishan's one-time blast to GHL contacts (428+) when giveaway launches
- Ongoing entries from the Typeform embed on the landing page

## Whose Job is What

- **VA (WhatsApp):** Entry confirmation, call invite messages, value drops, final check-ins. Uses scripts below in Ibrahim's voice ("I" not "Ibrahim will").
- **Ibrahim:** All calls (15-min shortlist + final). VA flags who booked.
- **VA does NOT:** Discuss pricing, promise winners, close sales, or book calls without Ibrahim confirming.

## Tier 1 Scripts (10/10 — Hot Leads)

### Day 1 — Entry Confirmation
> *"Thanks for entering the giveaway, [name]. I am going through every application personally. I will reach out in the next couple of days to learn more about you. Keep an eye on your messages."*

**WhatsApp only. No call yet.**

### Day 3-4 — Shortlist Call Invite
> *"Hey [name]. I am narrowing down who is in the running for the $5K spot. I want to hop on a quick 15-min call to hear your story directly. This does not guarantee you win, but it gets you shortlisted. Got time tomorrow?"*

**If they say yes:** Send GHL booking link.
**If no reply in 6 hours:**
> *"Still interested? I am finalising the shortlist tomorrow. Let me know if you want in."*

### Day 5 — First Value Drop
> *"Here is something to help while we go through the process — my top 3 nutrition rules my clients use to drop 5kg in the first month. Try them this week."*
Attach PDF guide.

### Day 7 — Second Value Drop
> *"Read this one — it is a quick story from a client who was exactly where you are 90 days ago. Thought it would hit for you."*
Share transformation story.

### Day 12-13 — Final Check
> *"Picking the winner in 48 hours. Jump on a quick call so I can make sure you are fully considered before I decide."*
Send booking link. Mark cold if no reply.

## Tier 2 Scripts (8-9/10 — Warm Leads)

### Day 1
> *"Thanks for entering, [name]. I am reviewing applications this week. While you wait, here is my top 3 nutrition rules to get you started."*
Attach PDF.

### Day 3-4
> *"How are you finding the guide? Quick question — what is the one thing holding you back right now?"*

### Day 5-6 — Call Push
> *"I am doing shortlist calls this week. 15 min to hear where you are at. Free tomorrow?"*

**If no reply in 2 days:**
> *"No worries if timing is not right. I have a July coaching package opening up at the giveaway discount price. Let me know if you want details."*

## Tier 3 Scripts (7 and Below — Nurture)

### Day 1
> *"Thanks for entering, [name]. Winner announced 7 July. While you wait, here is my guide to dropping 5kg in your first month."*

### Day 5-6
> *"Quick heads up — July coaching package opens next week at a special giveaway price. Limited spots. Want to hear about it?"*

### After Giveaway Ends
> *"The winner was announced. Keeping the $500 discount open for 48 hours for everyone who entered. Same 1:1 system. Want the details?"*

## VA Daily Checklist

1. Check Telegram for the daily lead summary
2. Note any new 10/10 entries — top of the list
3. Work through unanswered messages from yesterday
4. Send any scheduled Day X messages due today
5. Keep a simple tracking list: messaged, responded, booked call
6. Flag for Ibrahim: booked calls, strong vision answers
