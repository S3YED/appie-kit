# Coaching Business Audit Framework

Use when a client says "I have a mess that's making small money" or asks for a clear picture of their brand's current state vs what a successful brand looks like.

## The Audit Structure

### 1. Current State (9 Domains)

| Domain | What to assess |
|--------|----------------|
| **Offers** | How many tiers? Which are actually selling? Is pricing clear and consistent? |
| **Landing pages** | Live URLs? Designed to brand? Converting? |
| **VSL / sales video** | Drafted? Recorded? Published? Structured for conversion? |
| **Email** | Sequences? Nurture? Lead magnets? Automated? |
| **Social** | Calendar? Schedule? Strategy? Reactive or planned? |
| **Booking** | Funnel from lead to call? Standardised or ad-hoc? |
| **Testimonials** | Collected? Organised by result type? Placed on every touchpoint? |
| **Branding** | Colours locked? Applied everywhere? Consistent? |
| **Ads** | Running? Tracking? Profitable? |

### 2. Target State (What a £70k/mo brand has)

| Component | What's required |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Top of funnel** | Paid ads → VSL landing page + organic 3x/week + lead magnet |
| **Middle of funnel** | Email sequence (5-7 emails) + DM automation + retargeting |
| **Bottom of funnel** | Booking page + discovery call process + follow-up sequence |
| **Offer stack** | Cash cow (volume) → Core (main revenue) → Flagship (high profit) |
| **Delivery** | Not all 1:1. Group for cash cow. 1:1 for core. VIP for flagship. |
| **Social proof** | Testimonial bank by result type. Case studies for every touchpoint. |
| **Brand identity** | Colours, fonts, tone — locked and consistent. Recognisable in 0.5s. |
| **Operations** | VA/appointment setter. Content planned monthly. Metrics weekly. |
| **Metrics** | Cost per lead, conversion rates (lead→call→client), revenue per offer, churn. |

### 3. The Gap (The Creed example)

What the brand HAS vs what it NEEDS:

| Has | Needs |
|-----|-------|
| Results ✅ | System around them ❌ |
| Social proof ✅ | Organised and deployed ❌ |
| Brand name ✅ | Brand identity applied ❌ |
| ICP knowledge ✅ | Funnel targeting them ❌ |
| The coach ✅ | The coach scaled ❌ |

### 4. Build Checklist (30-Day Compression)

When the client wants to compress ALL phases into 1 month (not spread across 3-4 months):

| # | Deliverable |
|---|-------------|
| 1 | Brand style tile — colours locked, fonts set, logo variants ready |
| 2 | Cash cow landing page (VSL-based, deployed live) |
| 3 | Cash cow email sequence (5-7 emails) |
| 4 | Content calendar — reels scripts + captions + thumbnails |
| 5 | Testimonial bank — formatted for every touchpoint |
| 6 | Booking flow — Calendly link + reminder sequence |
| 7 | Objection-handling doc — for sales calls |
| 8 | VSL final script — recorded and ready |
| 9 | DM reply templates — common questions answered instantly |
| 10 | Month 2 plan — based on month 1 data |

### 5. Revenue Targets (The Creed example)

**Target:** £70k/month by month 3.

| Tier | Price | Volume needed | Monthly revenue |
|------|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| Cash Cow (Ignite) | £997 | 20-30 sales | £20-30k |
| Core (The Creed) | £3.5k-£6k | 5-8 sales | £20-40k |
| Premium (Emperor) | £10k-£15k | 1-2 sales | £10-30k |

**Month 1:** Build everything (compressed). **Month 2:** Drive traffic, test offers. **Month 3:** Scale to target.

## When to Use

Trigger phrases from client:
- "I don't really have a business, I have a mess"
- "Give me clarity on what I have vs what I need"
- "I want a full breakdown of current state"
- "What does a successful coaching brand look like?"
