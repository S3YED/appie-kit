# Ibrahim Ramzy — Brand Palette Reference

Last updated: 18 June 2026
Source: Session with Ibrahim — logo folder review + psychology research

## Master Brand Colours

| Role | Colour Name | Hex | Description |
|------|-------------|-----|-------------|
| Primary | Silver Metallic | `#B4B4BC` | Logo colour. Premium, modern, sleek. Quiet confidence. |
| Primary | Navy Blue | `#0F2D55` | Accent. Trust, authority, stability. Supports premium pricing. |
| Background | Dark Navy | `#072A42` | Default bg. Deep, serious, understated. |
| Optional | Royal Blue | `#004CC8` | Brighter accent option. Energy, dynamism. Better for ads/feeds. |

## NOT Approved
- Neon green (`#39FF14`) — strongly disliked
- Bright greens (`#00E676`, `#37CA37`)
- Gold (`#D4AF37`) — no longer desired
- Any colour that replaces or overpowers the silver logo — logo stays monochrome

## Colour Usage Rules (Landing Pages)
- **Logo:** Always all-silver (`#B4B4BC`) on dark background. Never recolour the logo itself. Brand colour lives AROUND the logo, not ON it.
- **Hero BG:** Dark Navy (`#072A42`). Silver logo on top.
- **Section BGs:** Alternate Dark Navy (`#072A42`) and Navy (`#0F2D55`).
- **Headings:** Silver (`#B4B4BC`) on dark sections, navy (`#0F2D55`) on light sections.
- **Body text:** White (`#FFFFFF`) or light gray on dark, dark gray on light.
- **Buttons/CTAs:** Royal Blue (`#004CC8`) with white text. This is the colour that drives action.
- **Accent lines/borders:** Silver metallic (`#B4B4BC`), thin.
- **Secondary accents:** Navy (`#0F2D55`) for less prominent interactive elements.

## Approved Colour Combinations

### Primary Brand Direction: Silver + Navy (CONFIRMED June 2026)
- **Logo:** Silver Metallic `#B4B4BC` on Dark Navy `#072A42` (all-silver, monochrome look — "still the best")
- **Primary accent:** Navy `#0F2D55`
- **CTA/button accent:** Royal Blue `#004CC8` (adds action/energy to interactive elements)
- **Background:** Dark Navy `#072A42`
- **Iconography:** Light silver/gray outlines on dark, navy fills on light

## FINAL APPROVED DIRECTION (Confirmed 19 June 2026)

| Element | Colour | Hex | Where Used |
|---------|--------|-----|------------|
| **Logo** | Silver Metallic | `#B4B4BC` | All logos — stays monochrome, never recoloured |
| **Primary brand colour** | Navy | `#0F2D55` | Section backgrounds, overlays, trust-building areas |
| **CTA/Buttons** | Royal Blue | `#004CC8` | Buttons, links, action elements (pops in feeds) |
| **Hero/section BG** | Dark Navy | `#072A42` | Main background, footer |
| **Body text on dark** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Readability |
| **Accent lines** | Silver Metallic | `#B4B4BC` | Borders, dividers, decorative lines |

**Logo rule:** Logo is always ALL SILVER (`#B4B4BC`) on a dark background. Never recolour the logo with blue or any other colour. The logo stays monochrome. Brand colour lives AROUND the logo (section backgrounds, buttons, accents), not ON it.

### Option A: Silver + Navy (for core + premium offers)
- Logo: Silver Metallic `#B4B4BC`
- Accents: Navy `#0F2D55`
- BG: Dark Navy `#072A42`
- Best for: The Creed (core £3.5k-£5k), Emperor (£10k-£15k)
- Psychology: Trust, premium, authority. Navy has the highest perceived trust of any blue.
- Used by: Premium automotive, luxury watches, financial institutions.

### Option B: Silver + Royal Blue (for ads/cash cow)
- Logo: Silver `#C0C0C0` (classic)
- Accents: Royal Blue `#004CC8`
- BG: Dark Navy `#072A42`
- Best for: Creed Ignite (cash cow, ad creative)
- Psychology: Energetic, dynamic, younger. Pops more in social media feeds.
- Trade-off: Less premium feel than navy. Better for volume than high-ticket.

### Option C: All Silver (monochrome)
- Logo: Light silver on dark
- Minimalist, Apple-like
- Risk: Loses warmth/emotional trigger. Only use for specific premium contexts.

## Brand Mockup Workflow
1. Load existing logo from Drive (01.png = black on transparent, 02.png = white on transparent)
2. Use PIL to recolor black/dark pixels with target colour
3. Place on dark navy canvas as hero mockup
4. Add accent line/stripe in the blue accent colour
5. Create palette card with hex codes for reference
6. Present options to client before building full pages
