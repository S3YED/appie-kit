## Direct Competitors (Men's Coaching)

### Physique Academy — The Armstrong Program
- **Page ID:** 1363182223828470
- **Website:** physiqueacademy.com
- **Target:** Men
- **Offer:** Multi-phase: Recomposition (rapid fat loss) → Reverse Engineering (maintenance) → Perform/Build/Sustain + Athlete Phase
- **Pricing:** Premium (1k-5k range)
- **Funnel:** Free 30-min consultation call. "Only apply if you're ready to take action."
- **Angle:** Mindset + body combined. Meditation, breathwork, sauna, ice baths alongside training. Community (Armstrong Army).
- **Ad Presence:** Moderate — runs FB ads with consult call CTA
- **Key Takeaway:** The "serious inquiries only" framing + multi-phase structure creates a premium feel. The mental health component (meditation, breathwork) is a strong differentiator.

### JM Transformations — James McCallion
- **Page ID:** 1015954634942014 ✅ Resolved

- **Location:** East Kilbride, Scotland

## Funnel Pattern Summary

| Funnel Type | Who Uses It | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Free call → coaching | Physique Academy, Danny Gates, Curtis High | Warm leads, premium pricing |
| Quiz → email → call | JM Transformations | Cold traffic, data collection |
| $1 trial → upsell | Funk Roberts | High volume, recurring revenue |
| Free PDF → email → launch | Coach Tregs | Content-first, low budget |
| Direct booking | Most common | Simple, works for most |

## What Ibrahim Can Learn from Each

- **From Physique Academy:** The "serious inquiries only" framing raises perceived value. Multi-phase structure creates upsell path.
- **From JM Transformations:** The quiz-funnel pre-qualifies before you touch the phone. Every objection surfaced in the form = a warmer call.
- **From Curtis High:** Free PDF on TikTok as a lead magnet is low friction and drives volume. Content-first builds trust before the pitch.
- **From Funk Roberts:** $1 trial is the ultimate low-friction entry. Community lock-in keeps retention high. Daily emails build habit.
- **From Coach James Club:** Medical/GLP-1 upsell as revenue expander. Subscription model for recurring revenue.
