| FAQ | Technique |
|-----|-----------|
| "What's the investment?" | Reframes as investment, not cost. No number given. Pushes to call. |
| "Does this work with a busy schedule?" | "We build around YOUR life, not the other way around." |
| "How does online work?" | App + weekly tracking + direct WhatsApp. Location list creates aspiration. |

### 10 Copy Techniques to Steal

1. Pain in headline — immediate identification
2. 3 objections killed in subheadline — diet, time, life upheaval
3. No price — always force to call
4. "Claim" not "buy" — lower resistance language
5. "Investment" not "cost" — reframes price as value
6. Specific timeframes on every result — "70 days", "45 days"
7. "They started where you are" — instant identification
8. Aspirational locations — "Amsterdam, Dubai, Bali"
9. Authority stacking — "doctor, psychologist, 10 years, 1000+ clients, 64 countries"
10. "Personal team" positioning — not a program, a team around you
