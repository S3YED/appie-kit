# Ibrahim Ramzy — ICP & Offer Knowledge Bank

Source: "Ibrahim ICP" Google Doc (based on 160 real customer surveys) + Exa deep research validation.

## Core ICP Profile

**Who:** Busy professionals, 25-35, UK/US/UAE, desk jobs, $60K-$120K/yr
**Gender:** 65% female, 35% male
**Occupation:** Healthcare (15%), tech/engineering (12%), business/corporate (20%), education (10%), legal/finance (8%), creative/entrepreneurial (10%)
**Status:** Single (40%), young families (30%), married no kids (30%)

## Deep Fear (3am thoughts)
- "I'm wasting my life being stuck in this body"
- "What if I never change and look back with regret?"
- "I'm letting myself down again and again"
- "I'm scared I'm not capable of being fit"

## Top Objections
1. "Will I actually stick to this?" → fear of self-trust failure
2. "I don't have time" → code for "I'm exhausted after work"
3. "Is it worth the money?" → past wasted money on programs
4. "Will this work for MY situation?" → PCOS, injuries, travel

## Exa Research Validation
External research confirms the ICP:
- **#1 theme across sources:** Execution, not knowledge. "Everyone knows what to do. Nobody does it."
- **Identity shift theory:** People who stay fit see themselves as "someone who works out" not "someone trying to work out"
- **Corporatize frame:** "The modern desk job is a biological paradox — brains running at million mph, bodies parked for 10 hours"
- **Market pricing benchmark:** Group coaching £200-500/mo, 1:1 hybrid £500-1,500/mo, premium £2k-5k/mo
