## Men ICP Language

### Pain Language (what they feel)
- "Body that avoids every mirror"
- "Confidence in the boardroom but not in the bedroom"
- "You run a company everyone wants to see but a body you hide"
- "My business is at 1%. My body is at average."
- "Tried every trainer in [city] and nothing worked"
- "I fall off every few weeks"
- "Overwhelmed by all the information out there"
- "I can't stay consistent"

### Dream Outcome Language (what they want)
- "Body that matches the bank account"
- "70 days and you could be next"
- "Finally proud of what I see in the mirror"
- "Leaner, sharper, carrying confidence out of the boardroom"
- "Walk past a mirror and finally smile"
- "The 1% get coached. The rest train alone."

### Style Rules
- **Short and sharp.** 10-15 words max per hook. Men stop scrolling fast.
- **Competitive framing** works: status, comparison, "your competitors are doing this"
- **Numbers work.** "70 days", "10kg", "25 minutes", "4 times a week"
- **Direct.** No softening. "You think you don't have time. 25 minutes says you do."

## Women ICP Language

### Pain Language (what they feel)
- "Hormones are all over the place"
- "Can't fit into my clothes anymore"
- "Using FaceApp to edit my photos before posting"
- "Choosing the angle that makes me look less fat"
- "Skipping meals like it's normal / almond-mom energy"
- "Having water for breakfast and calling it discipline"
- "Starting over every single Monday"
- "Put everyone else first. Leave myself for last."
- "I don't even recognise who I've become"
- "You miss her — the woman before the tiredness took over"
- "Dreading event photos. Avoiding group pictures."
- "Surviving on caffeine. Burnt out. On the edge."

### Dream Outcome Language (what they want)
- "Wear the dress that's been hiding at the back of the wardrobe for 2 years"
- "Finally walk into a room without dreading the photos"
- "Your kids copying your confidence instead of your insecurity"
- "Coming home with energy left for yourself, for once"
- "Not crashing on the couch as soon as you walk in"
- "Sleeping better. Hormones in check. In control again."

### Daughter / Identity Language
- **The Daughter Line (objection killer):** "You would never let your daughter speak about herself the way you do. So why are you teaching her that skipping meals is how women stay small?"
- **Dream outcome version:** "Your daughters are watching how you treat yourself. Here's how to be the mother they look up to — strong, confident, and finally done with hiding."

### Style Rules
- **Emotional depth matters.** The identity hook ("You miss her") outperforms blame hooks ("You don't prioritise yourself")
- **Specificity is key.** Women edit their photos. They choose angles. They skip meals. Call these specifics out.
- **Permission-giving language** works better than guilt-tripping. "It's not selfish. It's how you keep showing up for everyone else."
- **Say Goodbye format works well** when hyper-specific: "Say goodbye to FaceApp, hiding from photos, and choosing the less-fat angle."

## Faith-Driven ICP Language

### Pain Language
- "My body is an amanah and I'm not honouring it"
- "More consistent with Fajr than with my health"
- "Haven't been proud of my body since before COVID"
- "Career on point. Family taken care of. Deen on track. Body is the weakest pillar."
- "The guilt of knowing your body is a trust you're neglecting"

### Dream Outcome Language
- "Fitness as ibadah, not vanity"
- "Finally feel like the reflection matches the person inside"
- "25 minutes a day honours the body Allah gave you"
- "Ramadan doesn't derail progress — it powers it"
- "Strong enough to lead your family, serve your community, live your deen fully"

### Key Framing
- Reframe fitness from vanity → stewardship. "Taking care of your body isn't selfish. It's fulfilling a trust."
- Use their own consistency as proof they CAN do it. "You're already disciplined in every other area. Health is just the missing piece."
- The amanah frame is their language, not yours. Don't over-explain.

## How to Use This File

When writing ad hooks, objection handling scripts, VSL copy, or landing page headlines:

1. Pick the segment (Men / Women / Faith)
2. Start with the Pain language for the hook (stops the scroll)
3. Bridge to the Dream Outcome language (gets the click)
4. Inject the exact phrases verbatim — don't paraphrase
5. Run through Sabri Suby formula scaffolding (see `sabri-suby-hook-formulas.md`)

**Do NOT:** Write generic fitness copy. "Get in shape" is dead on arrival. The ICP language banks are what make the copy hit.
