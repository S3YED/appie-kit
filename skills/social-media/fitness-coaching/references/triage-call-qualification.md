# Triage Call Qualification Framework

Used for giveaway triage calls (15 min) where the coach's goal is to qualify leads for a second sales call. Not a pitch call — a selection call. Power dynamic: coach is selecting who DESERVES to win, not selling anything.

## Call Frame (Opening)

> *"Thanks for applying. This is simple — 15 minutes for me to hear your real story. Why you entered, what's actually going on, and why you think you should win the giveaway and how this is going to help you in your life. After today, I'll shortlist the people who are the best fit, and pick who I believe deserves it most."*

## Closing Frame

> *"Look, there are three winners. First place gets the full 12 weeks free. But even if you're not first — and I've never done this before — there's something special for second and third. My whole goal is to give back and change someone's life. So just be real with me in this call, and you're already ahead of most people."*

## 4 Qualification Questions

### Q1 — The Trigger (2 min)
> *"What happened recently that made you realise you have to sort your health out now?"*

**Green flags:** Specific event (out of breath with kid, blood test, missed milestone). Takes responsibility.
**Red flags:** Vague ("I've just been thinking about it"), no real trigger, blames circumstances.

### Q2 — The Life Audit (2 min)
> *"What do you do for work? And what does a normal day look like for you?"*

Assess income stability + schedule reality.
**Green flags:** Employed, business owner, consistent income. Can protect 30-60 min/day.
**Red flags:** Unemployed, chaotic schedule, "I'm really busy I don't know how I'd fit it in."

### Q3 — The Commitment Check (3 min)
> *"Be honest — have you signed up for programs before and not followed through? What happened?"*
> Follow-up: *"What's different this time?"*

Assess self-awareness vs excuses.
**Green flags:** Admits past failure. Knows they need accountability. Can name a specific change that makes NOW different.
**Red flags:** "No, I've always done it alone" (naive). Nothing has changed, just "feel more motivated." Blames the programs.

### Q4 — The Belief (3 min)
> *"Deep down, what do you actually think is the one thing holding you back?"*

**Green flags:** Honest about themselves ("I don't follow through when no one's watching," "I use work as an excuse").
**Red flags:** Blames external ("I need the right program"), vague ("I just need to eat better").

## Scoring

| Pattern | Score |
|---------|-------|
| Real trigger + stable life + takes responsibility + says something real | **HOT** — book second call |
| 2 of 4, others shaky | **WARM** — nurture sequence |
| Vague trigger + no stability + blames everything | **COLD** — no second call |

## Recording & Post-Call Workflow

After each call, Ibrahim sends: **Name / Score (H/W/C) / 1-line gut feel.**

### Recorded Calls → Lead Profiles

Ibrahim records every triage call on his end. Workflow:
1. He sends the recording or his call notes
2. Agent transcribes and builds a **lead profile document** for each person
3. Profile includes: trigger event, schedule/life reality, past program history, self-awareness level, commitment score
4. Use the profile to prep the second (sales) call — walk in knowing exactly what this person needs to hear

**Who does what:**
- Ibrahim: records calls, captures gut feel after each one
- Agent: transcribes recordings, builds profiles, organises by priority, sends him the second-call prep briefing

## Objection Handling (for questions they ask during triage)

### "What's the catch? Why free?"
> *"No catch. The giveaway is real — one person gets 12 weeks free. But honestly? Most people who enter aren't ready for real work. I'm looking for the one person who is. If that's not you, I'll offer you a way to work with me at a discount because I still want to help."*

### "What does the coaching actually involve?"
> *"It's not a meal plan and a PDF. It's me in your ear every day — training, nutrition, mindset. You follow the system. I hold the standard. 12 weeks, and you walk out different."*

### "How much time do I need?"
> *"45 minutes a day, 5 days a week. If you can't give me that, I can't give you the result. Fair?"*

### "Do you work with people like me?" (Muslim, busy, specific lifestyle)
> *"That's all I work with. People who have responsibilities — family, business, deen — and refuse to let their health be the thing that slips."*

### "I've tried programs and quit. How is this different?"
> *"Because I don't give you a program. I give you a standard. And I stay on you until you meet it."*

### "What if I don't win the giveaway?"
> *"Then I'll give you a way to work with me that still makes sense. The giveaway is for one person. The transformation is for whoever's ready."*

### "Can I do this with my schedule?"
> *"I've coached guys in active war zones. Your schedule is not the problem. Show up, I handle the rest."*

**Golden rule for every answer:** End with them wanting to hear more on the second call. You're not selling on the triage call — you're building enough trust that they show up to the next one.
