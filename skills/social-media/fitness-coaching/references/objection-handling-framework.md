# Objection Handling Framework — Fitness Coaching

## Core Distinction

| Type | Definition | Examples | Where Used |
|------|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Pain point** | Problem the program solves | "I can't stay consistent", "I don't know what to do", "I'm out of shape" | Ads, VSL, landing page copy |
| **Objection** | Reason they give NOT to invest | "I need to speak to my partner", "Now's not the right time", "It's too expensive" | Objection handling videos, sales call |

**Rule:** If the program directly fixes it, it's a pain point, not an objection. Objections are the fears that stop a sale even when the prospect wants the result.

## 5 Categories of Real Objections

### 1. Money & Value
- "It's too expensive / I can't afford this" — fear of wasting money
- "I need to check my finances first" — fear of overcommitting
- "I'm just looking / researching options" — paralysis by analysis
- "I've been burned before — coaches are all talk" — trust broken

### 2. Decision Authority
- "I need to speak to my partner / family" — fear of making wrong call alone
- "Let me think about it" — fear of impulsive mistake

### 3. Timing
- "Now's not the best time / I'm too busy" — fear of disruption + failure
- "I'll start after Ramadan / holiday / project" — perfectionism + delay

### 4. Skepticism
- "Online coaching doesn't work" — mistrust in delivery model
- "Nothing works for me" — hopelessness
- "I can do this on my own" — ego + self-reliance

### 5. Faith & Identity
- "Is this halal?" — misalignment with values
- "What if I don't stick with it?" — fear of self-betrayal

## Video Script Template (60-90s each)

```
[Hook — :00-:10] Name the objection directly
"People tell me [OBJECTION]. I get it."

[Story — :10-:25] Client had the exact same worry
Name + profession + situation

[Pivot — :25-:40] Reframe
"What they realised was..."

[Proof — :40-:55] Specific numbers
"[Client] lost X kg / gained Y kg in Z weeks"

[CTA — :55-:60+] Direct action
"If that sounds like you, apply below"
```
