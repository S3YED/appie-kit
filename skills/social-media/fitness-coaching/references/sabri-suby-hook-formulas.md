## 4 Timeless Headline Formulas

**Formula 1**
```
X ways to [desirable thing] without [undesirable thing]
```
→ "6 ways to get washboard abs without doing a single sit-up"

**Formula 2**
```
Do [difficult thing] in [specific period of time], even if [shortcomings]
```
→ "Pay off your mortgage in 7 years even if you have a modest income"

**Formula 3**
```
Achieve [desired thing] like [expert], even without [something expected]
```
→ "Paint a masterpiece like Picasso without ever having a single lesson"

**Formula 4**
```
How to eliminate [biggest problem] without [thing they hate] within [specific time frame]
```
→ "How to eliminate your muffin top without giving up the foods you love within 21 days"

> "You can simply take these formulas, swipe and deploy them, and they will work."

## 5 Fascination (Bullet/Headline) Formulas

**Formula 5 — Challenge a Common Belief**
```
You need [X]. Wrong.
```
→ "You need to eat at a calorie deficit to lose weight. Wrong."
> "This creates massive intrigue because you're taking something your market has adopted as being true and you're saying it's all wrong."

**Formula 6 — Classic List**
```
[Number] ways to [outcome]
```
→ "5 ways to meet single women in Melbourne"
Also works as: "Where to find [Y]" / "How to eliminate [X]" / "How to stop [problem] forever"

**Formula 7 — What You Should Never Do**
```
What you should never do [in situation]
```
> "People are more compelled to know what they shouldn't do rather than what they should do — it taps into fear of loss."

**Formula 8 — Say Goodbye**
```
Say goodbye to [X frustration]
```
→ "Say goodbye to calorie counting and hour-long cardio sessions"
> "Something they're doing and don't like doing — you say they could say goodbye to it."

**Formula 9 — The Dirty Truth**
```
The [dirty] truth about [X] revealed
```
→ "The dirty truth about fish oil revealed"
→ "The dirty truth about property investment that no one would dare tell you"
> "Some of the biggest campaigns in history have this headline. It just continues to work."

## The 5 Essentials for Every Headline

| # | Essential | What It Means |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | **GRAB** | 3-4 word grabber. Doesn't need the full story. "It grabs them by the throat and demands attention." |
| 2 | **NUMBERS** | Opens a curiosity loop. Always use numbers. |
| 3 | **BURNING CURIOSITY** | Buzzwords, twists, address their objections right in the headline. |
| 4 | **BIG SPECIFIC BENEFIT** | The WIIFM — what's in it for me. Must be unmistakable. |
| 5 | **HYPER-SPECIFIC** | The more advertised-to your market, the more specific you need to be. |

> "Five times more people will read your headline than the rest of your copy. Once you've written your headline, you've already spent 80 cents of your dollar."

## Eyebrow Copy (Above the Headline)

```
Attention [specific ICP] who want [specific outcome]
```
→ "Attention property investors who want to become millionaires"

## The 17-Step Sales Message (for landing pages / long-form)

| # | Section | Framework |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 1 | Eyebrow Copy | "Attention [ICP] who want [outcome]" |
| 2 | Headline | Big bold specific benefit |
| 3 | Sub-Headline | Expand the promise, never tell the whole story |
| 4 | Irresistible Intrigue | Flame desire and curiosity in bullets |
| 5 | Floodlight on Pain | Describe problem in vivid detail. Kick their bruised knee. |
| 6 | Unique Solution | Dismantle alternatives. Show why yours is different. |
| 7 | Credentials | Case studies, testimonials, stats |
| 8 | Detail Benefits | For each feature, write 2 benefits |
| 9 | Social Proof | Numbers, reviews, video testimonials |
| 10 | Irresistible Offer | Value-packed, clear delivery of outcome |
| 11 | Sweeteners (Bonuses) | 3-4 bonuses, each worth the main price |
| 12 | Stack the Value | Total theoretical value vs your price |
| 13 | Reveal Price | Position as fraction of total value |
| 14 | Inject Scarcity | REAL scarcity only (capacity, stock, deadline) |
| 15 | Powerful Guarantee | Bear all the risk — lowers CAC more than it costs |
| 16 | Call to Action | Embedded command. Walk them through every step. |
| 17 | PS | Start negative, end positive. "Or don't... and keep suffering." |

## PS Formula (Closer)

```
Or don't [take action] and continue suffering with [their pain].
OR just [click/link] and [get the dream outcome].
```
