# Law-firm client call synthesis and branding workflow

Use this reference when a Weblyfe legal-services client call contains strategy, branding, content, operations, SEO, or proposal details mixed into a long transcript.

## 1. Sanitize first

Separate:
- project-relevant business/design facts;
- private family or relationship context;
- pricing/proposal/admin notes;
- sensitive credentials or personal data.

Do not dump the transcript into project docs. Create concise, usable project notes.

Recommended files:
- `client-content/call-insights-YYYY-MM-DD.md`
- `client-content/scope-delivery-plan.md`
- `assets/asset-brief.md`
- `client-content/seo-content-engine-plan.md`
- `client-content/intake-client-system-plan.md`
- `client-content/domain-launch-notes.md`

## 2. Branding comes before website polish

For Weblyfe legal projects, do not treat the first improved website draft as the final direction. Seyed’s expected process is:

1. Strategy/marketing call.
2. Client questions about services, process, audience, personality, and desired perception.
3. Moodboard.
4. Logo/brandmark exploration.
5. Color palette.
6. Typography.
7. Styleguide.
8. Website design/build.
9. SEO, tracking, Search Console, Google Business Profile.
10. Launch, instruction videos, support.

If the user says they have a specific logo process, stop before over-finalizing and ask for that SOP or examples.

## 3. Law-firm brand system structure

Create class-level brand docs rather than only editing the website:

- `branding/00-branding-process.md`
- `branding/01-brand-strategy.md`
- `branding/02-logo-exploration.md`
- `branding/03-moodboard-directions.md`
- `branding/04-brand-system.md`
- `branding/logo-concepts/prompt-bank.md`
- optional visual concept sheet in `branding/logo-concepts/`

Logo exploration should cover a system, not a single mark:
- full horizontal logo;
- stacked logo;
- wordmark only;
- monogram/brandmark only;
- light/dark versions;
- favicon/social avatar;
- business card/email signature application.

For legal clients, useful routes are:
- premium serif wordmark;
- compact monogram, e.g. initials;
- abstract mark inspired by clarity, guidance, documents, columns, or local architecture;
- restrained emblem only if it stays modern and scalable.

Avoid cliché legal symbols: gavels, scales, fake courtrooms, stock handshakes.

## 4. Asset brief is a deliverable

Ask for real assets early because they determine whether the site feels premium or generic.

Must-have:
- strong portrait of the lawyer;
- team photos if team is public;
- office/context photos;
- logo/source files;
- legal footer/compliance details.

Nice-to-have:
- simple horizontal iPhone videos for hero/banner motion;
- desk, documents, consultation room details;
- local city/business-context shots.

Add privacy warnings:
- no real confidential documents visible;
- no client-identifiable material;
- no AI processing of sensitive case data unless a separate safe architecture exists.

## 5. Website can be an operating system

Legal-service sites can include operational support pages, not just marketing pages:

- `/intake` with a multistep form;
- preparation pages for first consultation;
- document checklists by practice area;
- onboarding/information pages for existing clients.

For law firms:
- forms can email structured intake data;
- include confidentiality microcopy;
- do not request full sensitive dossiers in the first form;
- do not route sensitive intake through AI by default;
- clarify that contact or kennismaking does not automatically create an engagement.

## 6. Blog and SEO engine

A legal content engine is useful but must be approval-gated.

Process:
1. Agent researches topics and search intent.
2. Agent drafts article.
3. Lawyer reviews for legal accuracy.
4. Publish only after approval.
5. Track in Search Console/Analytics.

Good clusters:
- practice area + city;
- problem-oriented terms;
- process explainers;
- document/checklist pages.

Avoid thin city spam. Local pages need genuine local and service context.

## 7. Google Business Profile

For local legal clients, GBP is a high-impact quick win:
- create or claim profile;
- verify with client;
- add logo, photos, categories, services, opening hours;
- prepare ethical review request flow;
- never imply reviews or testimonials are available without client approval and compliance check.
