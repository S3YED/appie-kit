# Legal services website playbook

Use this reference when building or modernizing lawyer, advocate, legal consultant, or boutique law-firm websites for Weblyfe clients.

## Positioning pattern
A strong solo or boutique legal site should usually avoid pretending to be a large firm. If public evidence shows a small practice, turn that into an advantage:
- direct contact with the lawyer;
- personal attention;
- clear process;
- confidentiality;
- local presence;
- careful expectations about costs and engagement.

For Dutch legal clients, a strong positioning shape is:
> Premiumer and more trustworthy than lead-gen legal sites, but more personal and accessible than large corporate firms.

## Research checklist
Before designing:
1. Audit the current site visually and technically.
2. Extract practice areas, contact details, terms, footer/legal pages, sitemap, metadata and schema.
3. Check public lawyer or professional-register listings where applicable, such as NOvA for Dutch advocates.
4. Check business registry facts where public, such as KVK in the Netherlands.
5. Search public case databases if relevant, but treat no results as a neutral fact, not a negative signal.
6. Research competitors in two groups:
   - local premium firms for authority/design patterns;
   - high-conversion niche firms for intake/CTA/SEO patterns.
7. Separate sourced facts, user-provided context and inference.

## Dutch law-firm trust and compliance notes
For Dutch advocate websites, use restraint and avoid misleading impressions.

Add or plan for a discreet `Kantoorinformatie` or legal footer section containing, where accurate:
- contracting party;
- NOvA registration or professional-register details;
- registered practice areas if applicable;
- professional liability insurance statement;
- complaints procedure;
- third-party funds / derdengelden statement;
- KVK/contact details;
- privacy and cookie links.

Avoid:
- claiming a large team when it is a solo practice;
- vague or unsupported specialist claims;
- guarantees about outcomes;
- aggressive pressure CTAs;
- public testimonials about sensitive matters unless clearly appropriate and consented.

## Conversion pattern
Law-firm conversion should be calm, confidential and low-pressure.

Recommended CTA hierarchy:
- Primary: `Plan een vertrouwelijke kennismaking` or `Vraag vrijblijvend advies aan`.
- Secondary: `Bel direct`.
- Tertiary: `Stuur uw vraag` or `Vertel kort uw situatie`.

For booking, prefer a controlled appointment request over a public calendar that asks for sensitive case details. Add microcopy:
> Een kennismaking betekent nog niet dat er een opdrachtrelatie ontstaat. Na controle op belangenconflicten en duidelijke afspraken ontvangt u een opdrachtbevestiging.

For forms:
- ask for minimal facts first;
- include preferred contact method;
- include practice-area dropdown;
- add confidentiality guidance near the form;
- do not invite full dossier uploads in the first form unless the intake flow is secure and intentional.

Useful form microcopy:
> Deel via dit formulier nog geen zeer gevoelige of volledige dossierinformatie. Wij nemen contact met u op via een passende route.

## Homepage structure
Recommended order:
1. Hero with clear legal positioning, location and confidential CTA.
2. Trust strip: registered lawyer, confidentiality, local office, clear costs/process.
3. Problem-based practice area cards.
4. Process: intake, analysis, options, written engagement.
5. Lawyer profile and credentials.
6. Costs and expectations.
7. FAQ around confidentiality, engagement, costs, first meeting and documents to bring.
8. Contact block with phone, email, address and short form.
9. Legal footer / kantoorinformatie.

## SEO page map
For local Dutch law firms, create service + location pages only where the practice genuinely handles that work. Avoid thin city spam.

Examples:
- letselschade advocaat Rotterdam;
- verkeersongeval Rotterdam;
- bedrijfsongeval advocaat;
- smartengeld claimen;
- arbeidsrecht advocaat Rotterdam;
- ontslag advocaat Rotterdam;
- vaststellingsovereenkomst controleren;
- bestuursrecht advocaat Rotterdam;
- bezwaar tegen besluit gemeente;
- contractrecht advocaat Rotterdam;
- contract laten beoordelen.

Use schema where appropriate:
- LegalService;
- Attorney;
- LocalBusiness;
- FAQPage;
- BreadcrumbList.

## Visual direction
Good law-firm visuals:
- real lawyer portrait;
- office / consultation setting;
- documents, notebook, pen, architectural context;
- local city cues, used subtly;
- deep navy, warm ivory, charcoal, restrained gold or bronze;
- elegant serif paired with readable sans-serif.

Avoid:
- gavels;
- scales of justice clichés;
- fake courtrooms;
- stock handshakes;
- dramatic crisis imagery;
- cold corporate sameness.

## First concept QA
A good first internal concept should pass:
- build and lint;
- local browser smoke test;
- above-the-fold visual QA;
- nav/CTA visibility check;
- no obvious missing metadata;
- honest notes on placeholders and assets still needed.

If browser visual QA flags large empty first-scroll bands, treat it as a content-density issue, not just a spacing issue. Add real module content or stronger visual structure before showing the concept externally.
