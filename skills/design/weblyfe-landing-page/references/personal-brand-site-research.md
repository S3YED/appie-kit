# Personal brand / creator site research notes

Use this when the client site is not a simple service landing page but a creator, influencer, or expert brand with multiple funnels.

## Pattern found in Nathan Nuyts session
- Existing domain `nathannuyts.com` functioned as a 6 Figure Creator funnel.
- New Webflow site was still mostly the Olivia template, so inspect carefully for what is template vs real brand content.
- Instagram position read as `Creator | Model`, so the site should reflect lifestyle + authority, not only coaching.
- Drive assets were organized into reusable groups:
  - `AI Images` for atmosphere/backgrounds
  - `Brand Partners` for proof logos
  - `Figma exports` for banners, testimonials, CTAs, profile assets
  - `FAQ CSV` for funnel copy
- Tony Robbins was the right structural reference: authority ecosystem, many pathways, proof-first, big navigation, programs, events, podcast/content.

## Recommended discovery sequence
1. Audit the live site and separate template content from real brand assets.
2. Inspect the social profile for positioning, follower scale, post themes, and collab language.
3. Review Drive / asset folders and categorize images into proof, lifestyle, CTA, background, and logo groups.
4. Compare the brand to a structural model site, not to copy visuals literally.
5. Decide whether the homepage is a single funnel or a multi-path personal brand hub.

## Useful homepage model for creator brands
- One hero promise.
- 3 clear pathways for different intents.
- Proof stack immediately below hero.
- Program / offer ecosystem after proof.
- Content or authority section for scale.
- CTA repeated with different levels of commitment.

## Pitfalls
- Don't assume a polished domain means a custom build. It may still be a template.
- Don't collapse a creator brand into one CTA if the brand has multiple monetization paths.
- Don't ignore Instagram voice and collabs. For creator brands, social identity is part of the site architecture.
- Don't use the competitor/reference site for visual copying. Use it to learn structure and hierarchy.
