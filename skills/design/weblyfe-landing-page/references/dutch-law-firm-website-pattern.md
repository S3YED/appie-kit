# Law-firm website discovery and proposal pattern

Use this reference when building a website/proposal for a Dutch law firm or advocaat.

## Discovery sources
- Current website: service pages, contact page, terms, privacy, sitemap.
- NOvA Zoek een advocaat: public lawyer profile, district, oath/admission date, firm size, public registered practice areas, specialist memberships if shown.
- KVK public search: legal form, address, trade names, KVK number.
- Rechtspraak.nl: search lawyer/firm name for public decisions, but report absence carefully as `no public results found during this pass`, not as proof of no cases.
- Competitors: large local firms, niche practice-area firms, and conversion-heavy lead-gen firms.

## Positioning pattern
For a solo or small law office, do not fake scale. Turn smallness into trust:
- direct contact with the advocaat;
- personal attention;
- clear process and cost expectations;
- confidential handling;
- local accessibility.

## CTA pattern
Use controlled conversion, not aggressive lead-gen:
- primary: `Plan een vertrouwelijke kennismaking` or `Vraag vrijblijvend advies aan`;
- secondary: `Bel direct`;
- tertiary: `Stuur uw vraag` or `Vertel kort uw situatie`.

If booking is included, prefer an appointment request over an exposed calendar that asks for sensitive case details. Add microcopy that a kennismaking is not yet an opdrachtrelatie and that engagement follows conflict checks and written agreements.

## Compliance/trust content
Include or reserve room for:
- full name and title, e.g. `mr. F. Soleiman, advocaat`;
- NOvA registration / rechtsgebiedenregister where accurate;
- KVK and office details;
- complaints procedure;
- beroepsaansprakelijkheidsverzekering statement;
- derdengelden statement;
- privacy and cookie links;
- no outcome guarantees or unsupported specialist claims.

## Imagery
Prefer real portrait and office/context photography. Avoid gavels, scales, fake courtrooms, stock handshakes and exaggerated crisis imagery. If no images exist yet, use abstract premium art direction and mark portrait/office assets as a requirement before client presentation.

## SEO map
Build pages around service + location and problem-language combinations:
- letselschade advocaat Rotterdam;
- verkeersongeval / bedrijfsongeval / smartengeld;
- arbeidsrecht advocaat Rotterdam;
- ontslag / vaststellingsovereenkomst / loonvordering;
- bestuursrecht advocaat Rotterdam;
- bezwaar tegen besluit / vergunning / handhaving;
- contractrecht advocaat Rotterdam;
- contract laten beoordelen.

## Quality gate
Before presenting to the client, verify:
- no typo in legal practice labels or URLs;
- no misleading `wij` or team implication for solo firms;
- hero has a visible CTA;
- contact form has confidentiality/privacy microcopy;
- legal footer placeholders are either filled or clearly flagged as pending.
