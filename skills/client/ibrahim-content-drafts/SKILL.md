# Ibrahim Content Drafts

## When to Use

Use when Ibrahim asks for content: Instagram Reels scripts, captions, carousel posts, stories, ad copy.

## Brand Voice Rules

1. No em dashes. Periods and commas only.
2. No metaphors, no flowery language.
3. Short, punchy sentences. One idea per sentence.
4. Sound human. Read it aloud — if it sounds robotic, rewrite.
5. Hit the deeper pain. Surface problems are symptoms.
6. Structure: Hook -> Tension -> Real Talk -> Action -> CTA
7. Use "everyone" not "guys."
8. Transformation photo always prominent.

## Reel Script (0-30s)

**0-3s Hook:** Stop-scroll question or statement about ICP's pain.
**3-15s Tension:** Name the struggle. "You eat clean, train hard, but something's off."
**15-25s Pivot:** The shift. "It's not your workout. It's your recovery."
**25-30s CTA:** "Start with this tonight. DM me CREED for the full system."

**Hook Templates:**
- "You're doing everything right and still stuck. Here's why."
- "The golden cage has a price. Your health."
- "Talabat three times a week and wondering why your body isn't changing?"
- "I train Muslim high-performers who can't seem to break through."
- "Your salary went up. Your strength didn't."

## Caption Structure

1. Opening line that hooks
2. Body (2-4 sentences of real talk)
3. CTA (DM, save, share)

**Example:**
"You're ordering Talabat because you have no time. But you have time to scroll for an hour.
That's not a time problem. That's a priority problem.
The golden cage is comfortable. But comfort doesn't build discipline.
Want the system that fixes this? DM me CREED."

## Carousel (10 slides)

1. Title slide + Ibrahim's photo
2. The problem (1-2 sentences)
3. The real reason (deeper driver)
4. The reframe
5-7. Three actionable steps
8. Result + transformation photo
9. CTA
10. Final slide: "DM me CREED"
