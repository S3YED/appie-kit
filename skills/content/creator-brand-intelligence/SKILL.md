# Creator Brand Intelligence

Research, audit, and build outreach for brand partnership opportunities tailored to a creator's niche, audience, and existing portfolio.

## When to Activate

- User asks what brands they should work with or what categories they're missing
- User wants an outreach list with emails and contact methods
- User wants to know which brands do paid recurring deals in their niche
- User says "find me brands to partner with", "brand deal opportunities", "sponsorship list", "collab outreach"
- Auditing existing brand portfolio for gaps
- Researching specific brand categories (men's clothing, SaaS tools, fintech, etc.)

## Workflow

### Phase 1: Portfolio Audit

Start by mapping what the creator already has:

1. **Extract existing brand portfolio** from whatever source is available (Cognify knowledge graph, memory, website, the user themselves)
2. **Categorize by industry** — fashion, beauty, tech, travel, fitness, entertainment, etc.
3. **Identify the creator's core niches** — what content do they make? Who is their audience? What's their personal brand?
4. **Map gaps** — categories that fit their niche but have zero brand deals

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What content do you create? | Determines which brands are a natural fit |
| Who is your audience? | Brands care about demo match |
| What's your vibe/aesthetic? | Premium, streetwear, business, casual? |
| Where do you post? | Some brands only do IG, others do YT or TikTok |
| Past deals (even organic)? | Shows what categories already work |

### Phase 2: Category Discovery

For each gap category, identify sub-niches and brand candidates:

Common creator categories:
- **SaaS & Creator Tools**: Notion, Canva, Descript, Riverside, ConvertKit, Kajabi, Airtable, Adobe
- **Fintech/Finance**: Revolut, Wise, Stripe, PayPal, crypto platforms
- **Travel** (digital nomads): Airbnb, Booking.com, Skyscanner, travel insurance
- **Camera & Gear**: DJI, RØDE, Peak Design, Sony, Elgato
- **Productivity/Desk Setup**: Logitech, Secretlab, Herman Miller, monitors
- **Health & Wellness**: Calm, Headspace, Oura Ring, Eight Sleep, supplements
- **AI Tools**: ChatGPT, Midjourney, Opus Clip, Notion AI
- **Watches & Accessories**: Daniel Wellington, TAG Heuer, Tissot
- **Coffee/Energy**: Celsius, Nespresso, matcha brands
- **Education Platforms**: Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific, Podia
- **Men's Clothing**: Represent, Carhartt WIP, AllSaints, Ralph Lauren, Tommy Hilfiger, Diesel, Fear of God, Kith, Aime Leon Dore, Uniqlo, Zara Men, Scotch & Soda, Paul Smith, Nordstrom

### Phase 3: Contact Research

For each brand, find the best contact channel. Common patterns:

| Type | How to find | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct email** | Search brand website, press pages, help center | `partnerships@ouraring.com`, `KOL@influencer.dji.com`, `strategic-partnerships@canva.com` |
| **Ambassador program** | Usually a form on the brand's site | `danielwellington.com/pages/become-an-ambassador` |
| **Affiliate program** | Via affiliate networks (AWIN, CJ, Partnerize) or brand site | `notion.com/affiliates`, `levi.com/affiliate-program` |
| **Sponsorship form** | Brand's "sponsorship" or "partnerships" page | `secretlab.co/pages/sponsorship`, `celsius.com/ambassador` |
| **Creator program** | Brand's help center or creator-specific page | `riverside.com/creator-partnerships`, `logitech.com/influencers-creators-program` |
| **PR/gifting** | Pitch via email, usually works for micro-creators | `joe@eightsleep.com` (Eight Sleep) |

### Phase 4: Verification & Prioritization

Before presenting the list:

1. **Verify the brand actually does paid deals** — not just gifting/PR. Check Modash, search "brand x influencer sponsored" or check their creator program page
2. **Prioritize by fit** — how aligned is the brand with the creator's content and audience?
3. **Prioritize by ease** — direct email > form > affiliate network (affiliate is easiest to start but pays less)
4. **Flag deal type** — paid sponsorship, affiliate commission, gifting-only, ambassador retainer

## Contact Research Techniques

When searching for brand contacts:

1. **Search patterns** — try multiple queries per brand:
   - `"brand name" influencer partnership email`
   - `"brand name" creator program ambassador`
   - `"brand name" sponsorship partnership contact`
   - `site:brand.com partnership OR ambassador OR influencer`

2. **Extract from help centers** — brand help centers often have partnership sections

3. **Use Modash** — check how many creators a brand has sponsored (sign of paid deal activity)

4. **Check affiliate networks** — CJ Affiliate, AWIN, Partnerize, Impact — many brands are only reachable through these

5. **Check social media** — brand's Instagram/TikTok often shows recent collabs; DM the brand's partnerships team

## Output Format

Present outreach lists in a table with:

| # | Brand | Category | Contact | Type | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brand Name | Category | `email@brand.com` or [link to form](url) | Paid / Affiliate / Ambassador | High/Med/Low |

Include a "top 5 to pitch first" section with reasoning.

## Pitfalls

- **Don't assume PR/gifting = paid**. Many brands send free product but don't pay. Verify before listing as a paid opportunity.
- **Don't recommend brands the creator already works with**. Check their portfolio first.
- **Don't guess emails**. Verify via brand websites. Wrong emails bounce and waste the creator's time.
- **Forms often go to a black hole**. Direct emails have higher conversion than forms. Affiliate programs are the easiest entry point.
- **Some brands only work through agencies**. For premium/luxury brands, find the agency contact instead.
